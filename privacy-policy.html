Lentia Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Summary: Lentia does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. There are no user accounts, servers, analytics, advertising or trackers. Everything you record stays on your own devices.

1. What data we collect

None. Lentia does not request, collect or transmit any personally identifiable information: no name, email address, phone number, device identifiers, location or health data from Apple Health.

2. What the app stores and where

Lentia stores the following only on your device, in a storage area shared between the app and its widgets:

  • The contact lenses you log for each eye: brand, the prescription values you choose to enter, replacement schedule, the date you opened them, lot and expiry.
  • The times you put your lenses in and take them out.
  • Your spare lens stock, lens solution, lens case and check-up dates.
  • Your settings (reminder times, maximum hours, language, appearance, your shop's link).

This information is not sent to any server, not shared with third parties and not accessible to the developer. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

3. Apple Watch

If you use the Apple Watch app, your iPhone sends the current state of your lenses directly to your paired watch, and the watch sends back when you put your lenses in or take them out. This uses Apple's device-to-device connection between your own iPhone and Apple Watch; no server of ours is involved.

4. Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. They are not sent through any server.

5. Internet connection

Lentia works entirely offline. The only time it uses the network is if you tap the link to your own lens shop, which opens it in your browser.

6. Third parties

Lentia does not include any advertising, analytics, social network or tracking SDK. No third party has access to your information.

7. Children

Lentia is not directed at children under 13 and, as it collects no data, it cannot collect information from minors.

8. Payments

Lentia is a paid app with a single purchase made through the App Store. It has no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; the developer never receives your payment details.

9. Your rights

Since no data is collected, there is nothing to request, rectify or delete on our side. You can delete everything the app stores by uninstalling it from your iPhone and Apple Watch.

10. Not medical advice

Lentia helps you keep track of your contact lenses. It is not a medical device and it is not medical advice. Always follow the instructions of your optician or eye doctor and of the makers of your lenses and lens solution.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
